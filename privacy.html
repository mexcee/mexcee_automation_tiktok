Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Who we are

System Automatize TikTok ("the Service") is a personal, non-commercial project run by an individual administrator. It uses a self-hosted instance of Postiz, open-source social media management software, installed on the administrator's own computer.

2. Information we use

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok asks you to approve the requested permissions. Depending on what you approve, the Service may receive:

  • Profile information: your TikTok account identifier (open_id), username, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Account statistics: follower count, following count, likes count and video count, used to display basic analytics to the account owner.
  • Video information: the list of your videos (identifier, title, cover image, link) and their view, like, comment and share counts, used to show the result of publications and their analytics.
  • Authorization tokens issued by TikTok, used to act on your account only within the permissions you approved.
  • Content you provide, such as videos, captions and publication settings (for example, privacy level or schedule).

The Service does not request your TikTok password. Sign-in happens on TikTok's own pages.

3. How we use it

  • To display the connected account in the Service.
  • To upload and publish the videos and captions you choose, at the time you request or schedule.
  • To check the publication status and show the account owner statistics about their account and published videos.

This information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for any purpose other than managing your connected accounts and the publications you request.

4. Where it is processed

  • Postiz (self-hosted): account information, tokens, posts and media are stored in the Postiz database and storage on the administrator's computer.
  • TikTok: content you publish is sent to TikTok and is then handled according to the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Network provider: the Service may be reached through a tunnel or hosting provider (such as Cloudflare), which carries traffic between your browser and the Service.

5. How long it is kept

Information is kept while the account remains connected to the Service, or until the administrator deletes it. Content already published on TikTok stays on TikTok until you delete it there.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the Service's access at any time from the TikTok app, in the settings for apps and services connected to your account. You can also disconnect the account in Postiz. After revocation, the Service can no longer publish on your account.

7. Deleting your data

You can ask the administrator to delete the information stored locally about your account (see Contact below). The administrator can remove the connected account, its tokens and related posts from the self-hosted Postiz instance.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to restrict access to the Service. However, no system is completely secure.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children and should only be used by people allowed to use TikTok under its own rules.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be submitted through the GitHub Issues of this project. Issues are public: please do not include sensitive personal information. Describe your request and the administrator will reply there.

See also the Terms of Service.
